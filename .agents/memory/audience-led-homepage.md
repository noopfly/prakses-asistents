---
name: Audience-led homepage
description: Product positioning guidance for the Prakses Asistents homepage and its specialty switch.
---

The homepage should lead with the active clinician audience, especially the family-practice path, rather than introducing the product generically and revealing the audience later. The GP experience is the primary story; endocrinology must remain a clearly separate specialty path with its own features, proof points, and CTA. The compact two-pill audience label in the hero is visual-only; the actual role switch belongs in the functionality section.

**Why:** The original functionality selector made the homepage feel visually separated and did not make it clear that the product was for GPs. Making the audience visible in the hero and carrying the same selection into the feature section creates a coherent narrative.

**How to apply:** Keep the functional role state synchronized with the functionality content, proof, and final CTA. Use the hero pills as a static audience cue, not a clickable control. Use audience-specific hero copy and product-preview labels, and avoid showing GP-specific proof or labels when endocrinology is selected.
