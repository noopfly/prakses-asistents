---
name: Audience-led homepage
description: Product positioning guidance for the Prakses Asistents homepage and its specialty switch.
---

The homepage should lead with the active clinician audience, especially the family-practice path, rather than introducing the product generically and revealing the audience later. The GP experience is the primary story; endocrinology must remain a clearly separate specialty path with its own features, proof points, and CTA.

**Why:** The original functionality selector made the homepage feel visually separated and did not make it clear that the product was for GPs. Making the audience visible in the hero and carrying the same selection into the feature section creates a coherent narrative.

**How to apply:** Keep role selection synchronized across the page. Use audience-specific hero copy, product-preview labels, functionality content, value proof, and final CTA. Avoid showing GP-specific proof or labels when endocrinology is selected.
